A Space to Think

Step Away
from the Ordinary

Riad Mayo offers an entirely private estate for groups that need to step away from the office, the city and the noise. Set on 5 hectares of private land in the Ourika Valley, the estate provides space to gather, think, create and reconnect — without distraction.

Whether you are planning a corporate off-site, a wellness retreat, a creative residency or a leadership programme, the estate can be arranged to support your group's goals — from the workspace configuration to the daily schedule, dining and excursions.

Our concierge team handles all logistics so your focus remains on the work, the rest, or both.

Meeting &
Workshop Spaces

The estate's indoor and outdoor spaces can be arranged to support working sessions, presentations, workshops and group discussions. The riad's main salon, garden terraces and outdoor seating areas are all available for use throughout the day.

  • Main riad salon for plenary sessions
  • Garden terrace breakout spaces
  • Outdoor workshop setup in the olive grove
  • AV equipment available on request
  • Flexible seating arrangements
  • Quiet writing or working rooms within the riad
Wellbeing

Wellness
Programming

For groups seeking a restorative or wellness-focused retreat, the estate can be arranged to support daily yoga sessions, hammam treatments, massage therapies and guided relaxation. The pool, gardens and outdoor spaces are conducive to slow, intentional rest.

  • Daily yoga sessions in the garden or pool terrace
  • Traditional Moroccan hammam treatments
  • Professional massage therapy
  • Guided meditation & breathwork sessions
  • Morning movement practices
  • Nutritional dining programmes on request
At the Table

Team Dining
Experiences

Shared meals are often the most valuable part of any retreat. All dining is prepared by Chef Rabia and her team, with menus designed around the rhythm and focus of your group. From working lunches and communal dinners to celebratory feasts at the end of a week's programme.

  • Retreat menus designed with Chef Rabia
  • Working lunches & seminar catering
  • Family-style communal dinners
  • Moroccan feast evenings
  • Cooking classes & culinary experiences
  • Dietary requirements accommodated in full
Beyond the Estate

Cultural
Excursions

The Ourika Valley and surrounding region offer a rich range of excursions for groups looking to explore beyond the estate. Our concierge team can arrange private guided experiences tailored to your group's interests.

  • Guided Atlas Mountain hiking
  • Agafay Desert camel rides & quad biking
  • Marrakesh Medina & souk tours
  • Ourika Valley waterfall walks
  • Private visits to local artisan workshops
  • Hot air balloon experiences over the plains
