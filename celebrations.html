Mark the Moment

A Private Estate
to Celebrate

Life's most meaningful milestones deserve a setting that rises to meet them. At Riad Mayo, the full estate is yours — the gardens, pool, terraces and riad spaces — exclusively reserved for your group for the duration of your stay.

Whether you are celebrating a significant birthday, an anniversary, an engagement or a private gathering of people you love, the atmosphere can be shaped entirely around the occasion — from the dining and entertainment to the decorations and flow of the evening.

Our team takes care of every detail, so your only role on the day is to enjoy it.

The Evening

Entertainment
& Performance

Celebrations at Riad Mayo can be as lively or as intimate as you choose. Our team can arrange a full evening of Moroccan performance and music, or create a quiet, candlelit atmosphere for something more understated.

  • Fire performances for outdoor evening events
  • Authentic Moroccan folk bands & Gnawa musicians
  • Belly dancers & traditional performers
  • DJ services for late-night celebrations
  • Acoustic musicians for dinner & cocktail hours
  • Custom playlists & sound system coordination
The Estate

Pool &
Garden Events

The estate's outdoor spaces — the pool terrace, olive grove, garden lawns and riad courtyard — are well suited to daytime and evening celebrations of all kinds. Pool parties, garden aperitivos, lantern-lit dinners beneath the trees.

  • Pool terrace events for up to 100 day guests
  • Olive grove garden receptions
  • Riad courtyard intimate gatherings
  • Outdoor dining tables & festive lighting
  • Daytime pool parties with catering
  • Sunset aperitivo hours on the terraces
At the Table

Custom Menus
& Cocktail Hours

All celebration dining is prepared by Chef Rabia and her team. Menus are composed around your group and the occasion — from canapé receptions and cocktail hours to long celebratory feasts and late-night grazing tables.

  • Bespoke celebration menus designed with Chef Rabia
  • Cocktail hours & welcome receptions
  • Moroccan feast banquets & sharing tables
  • Celebration cakes & dessert tables
  • Late-night grazing boards
  • Custom non-alcoholic & signature drink menus

  • Golden hour portrait sessions in the gardens
