Weddings & Celebrations

Events

Private Events at Riad Mayo

An Estate Reserved
for the Occasion

For a wedding, celebration or private gathering, Riad Mayo offers the freedom to make the occasion entirely your own. The house, gardens, pool and outdoor spaces come together as one private setting, allowing each event to unfold naturally across the estate.

From Moroccan wedding traditions and live folk music to candlelit dinners, garden receptions and evenings beneath the olive trees, every celebration can be shaped around the people, atmosphere and moments that matter most.

Our team works closely with each group to curate the setting, dining, entertainment and flow of the occasion, creating something personal, considered and distinctly Riad Mayo.

Events at Riad Mayo

Every event is designed around you — your vision, your people, your story.

Weddings

From intimate elopements to grand celebrations, Riad Mayo creates the backdrop for the wedding of a lifetime.

  • Ceremony & reception coordination
  • Florals, décor & lighting
  • Bespoke catering by Chef Rabia
  • Live music & entertainment
  • Full accommodation for your party

Celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries, engagements — mark life's milestones in a setting made for celebrating.

  • On-site entertainment — fire performances, authentic Moroccan folk bands & belly dancers
  • Custom menus & cocktail hours
  • Pool parties & garden events
  • DJ services available
  • Photography & videography support

Retreats

Corporate offsites, wellness retreats, creative residencies — a space to disconnect, focus, and reconnect.

  • Meeting & workshop spaces
  • Wellness programming on request
  • Team dining experiences
  • Cultural excursions
  • Full concierge support
