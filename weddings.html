The Wedding at Riad Mayo

A Setting Made
for the Occasion

Riad Mayo offers an entirely private estate for your wedding — a 5-hectare property set among olive groves and gardens in the Ourika Valley, 45 minutes from Marrakesh. The house, riad courtyard, gardens, pool terraces and outdoor spaces are yours exclusively for the duration of your stay.

Whether you envision a candlelit Moroccan ceremony beneath the olive trees, a grand reception around the pool, or an intimate gathering of your closest people, every element of your wedding can be shaped around you.

Our team works alongside you from the earliest stages of planning through to the last dance, coordinating every detail so the day unfolds exactly as you imagined.

Begin Planning
The Day

Ceremony &
Reception

The estate offers multiple outdoor ceremony settings — beneath the ancient olive trees, along the garden terraces, or beside the pool at golden hour. Indoor spaces within the riad provide an intimate alternative for smaller ceremonies.

Receptions can be arranged across the estate's outdoor grounds, with long candlelit tables, lanterns strung through the gardens, and the Atlas Mountains as backdrop.

  • Outdoor ceremony in the olive grove or gardens
  • Pool terrace receptions for up to 100 day guests
  • Riad courtyard intimate ceremonies
  • Candlelit garden reception tables
  • Full venue dressing & layout coordination
  • On-site coordination throughout the day
At the Table

Catering by
Chef Rabia

All dining is prepared by Chef Rabia and her team, whose cuisine draws from the deep traditions of Moroccan cooking — slow-cooked tagines, wood-fired breads, fragrant couscous and elaborate mezze — alongside Italian and Asian-inspired dishes.

Wedding menus are composed entirely around your group. Whether you prefer a formal seated dinner, a festive shared feast, or a cocktail reception followed by a late-night banquet, every aspect of the dining experience can be tailored.

  • Bespoke wedding menus designed with Chef Rabia
  • Traditional Moroccan feasts & sharing tables
  • Formal plated dinners for seated receptions
  • Cocktail hours & canapé service
  • Wedding cake coordination
  • Late-night snacks & post-celebration dining
Atmosphere

Florals, Décor
& Lighting

The natural beauty of the estate — jasmine-scented gardens, ancient olive trees, terracotta walls and tiled courtyards — forms the foundation of your wedding aesthetic. Our team can assist in coordinating décor, florals and lighting to complement the setting.

  • Floral coordination with local Moroccan florists
  • Lantern, candle & ambient lighting arrangements
  • Table styling, linen & tableware
  • Ceremony arch & aisle dressing
  • Outdoor lighting throughout the estate
  • Welcome arrangements & guest suite florals
The Evening

Music &
Entertainment

Evenings at Riad Mayo take on a rhythm of their own. From traditional Moroccan folk musicians and Gnawa performers to contemporary DJs and acoustic sets, the entertainment can be shaped entirely around the atmosphere you want to create.

  • Authentic Moroccan folk bands & Gnawa musicians
  • Belly dancers & traditional performers
  • Fire performances for evening receptions
  • DJ services for late-night celebrations
  • Acoustic musicians for ceremony & dinner
  • Sound system & lighting coordination
Staying On

Accommodation
for Your Party

The estate accommodates up to 30 overnight guests across 8 bedrooms in the main riad and 4 private bungalows. Your group stays together, with the full estate reserved exclusively for your wedding party for the duration of your stay.

A minimum of 3 nights applies for all wedding bookings. Most couples choose to arrive the day before the ceremony, allowing time to settle in, rehearse and enjoy the estate at their own pace.

  • Up to 30 overnight guests across 8 rooms & 4 bungalows
  • Full exclusive use of the estate
  • Welcome hampers & suite preparation
  • Breakfast included each morning
  • Minimum 3-night stay for wedding bookings
  • Airport transfers available for all guests
