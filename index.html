A Privatised Retreat

A 5-Hectare Estate — Privatised, Secured, and Yours.

Riad Mayo & Bungalows is more than a uniquely private retreat — it is a family home with a story spanning generations. Rooted in the estate since the 1930s, it was long a place of family life, gatherings, and memories before opening its doors to guests in 2018 under the care of Naima Imouyarh.

Today, our boutique maison d'hôte offers a fully privatized experience, welcoming one group at a time into a place that has never lost the warmth and intimacy of a private home. Whether for families, friends, retreats, weddings, or special celebrations, each stay is designed to feel less like visiting a hotel and more like being welcomed into a home with history.

The Estate

Discover Riad Mayo

From the grandeur of the Riad House to the seclusion of four private bungalows — every corner of the estate invites total immersion.

The Riad House

A historic Moroccan residence dating to the 1930s, authentically preserved and generously proportioned. The Riad features 4 ensuite bedrooms, two rooftop terraces with sweeping 360° views, and a collection of traditional communal spaces — accommodating 12–15 guests across approximately 600 m².

Explore the Riad

The Bungalows

Four private ensuite bungalows set within lush greenery. Each 34 m² retreat sleeps up to 3 guests and features a rooftop terrace and chimney — secluded, self-contained, and serene.

The Gardens

Embraced by a centuries-old olive grove, the estate's 5 hectares unfold into distinct spaces for gathering, unwinding, and immersing in the natural beauty of the Ourika Valley.

The Pool

Reached through a connecting patio from the Riad, the estate pool stretches 15m × 7m beneath open sky and swaying palms — divided into a children's area and a deeper adult pool, lined with cabanas, sun loungers, and shaded retreats.

The Bird Sanctuary

Tucked within the gardens, the estate's bird sanctuary is home to peacocks and an assortment of resident birds — a small, much-loved part of daily life at Riad Mayo.

Activities

Throughout the estate, dedicated spaces invite guests to play, gather and spend time outdoors — from padel and basketball to table tennis, pool table, and relaxed garden activities.
